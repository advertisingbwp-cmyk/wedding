Wedding Website Dress Codes: How to Guide Your Guests with Elegance

One of the most frequent questions guests ask before attending a wedding is: "What should I wear?" When multi-day festivities include traditional rituals, outdoor daytime garden parties, and formal grand receptions, clear guidance removes all anxiety and ensures breathtaking event photographs.

1. Breaking Down Cultural Dress Codes

If you are welcoming guests from varied cultural backgrounds, spell out familiar terms alongside cultural wardrobe choices:

  • Haldi & Pithi: Shades of sunshine yellow, mustard, or marigold orange. Recommend comfortable cotton or raw silk fabrics that can handle turmeric splashes.
  • Mehendi: Vibrant jewel tones, mint greens, and boho florals. For women, 3/4 sleeves or sleeveless outfits are practical for wrist and arm henna.
  • Sangeet & Qawwali Night: High-glam traditional or fusion. Shimmering lehengas, Indo-western bandhgalas, or sequined cocktail sarees.
  • Nikah Ceremony: Modest elegance, soft pastels, ivory, and champagne embroidery. Kurta pajama with Nehru jackets or embellished sherwanis for men.
  • Vedic Mandap / Wedding: Traditional regal attire. Kanjeevarams, Benarasi silks, or royal sherwanis. (Note: White or black is often avoided in traditional Hindu rituals).
  • Walima / Grand Reception: Black-tie or formal royal. Tuxedos, tailored suits, evening gowns, or heavily embroidered velvet ensembles.

2. Providing Color Palette Swatches on Your Website

Rather than strict rules, modern luxury websites present suggested color swatches. In Riwaayat Venue, each ceremony card includes a visual palette swatch so guests can coordinate seamlessly with the aesthetic mood of the venue and floral decor.

Pro Host Tip: Comfort First

If part of the ceremony is on lawn grass or cobblestones, explicitly note: "Block heels or wedges recommended for the outdoor garden lawn." Your guests will deeply appreciate this thoughtfulness.

3. Clear FAQs Prevent Repetitive Inquiries

Adding a 2-question dress FAQ to your Riwaayat Venue website (e.g., "Where can out-of-town guests rent or buy traditional attire?" and "Is modesty required for sacred rituals?") gives guests complete confidence and peace of mind.
