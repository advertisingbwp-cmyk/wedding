The Destination Wedding Travel Hub: Maps, Shuttles & Room Blocks

Whether you are gathering family in Udaipur, Dubai, Istanbul, Tuscany, or Cancun, a destination wedding requires thoughtful logistics. Your website acts as the 24/7 digital concierge for every traveler, ensuring they arrive relaxed and prepared.

1. Hotel Room Block Codes & Booking Deadlines

Negotiated room block rates usually have an expiration date. Highlight your block code clearly with a direct booking hyperlink, group discount rate, and cutoff date prominently displayed at the top of your Travel section.

2. Airport Shuttle & Flight Details

If group shuttles will run from the airport to the resort, publish the departure schedule:

  • Nearest Airport: Provide airport name, airport code, and typical transit time to the resort.
  • Shuttle Frequency: E.g. "Private shuttles depart terminal 2 every 90 minutes between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM on Thursday."
  • Local Taxi Apps & Rates: Recommend trusted ride-hailing services or vetted car hire contacts.

3. Interactive Maps & Itinerary Reminders

Integrate Google Maps pins for venue gateways, welcome cocktails, and scenic spots. Riwaayat Venue lets you link venue pins directly so guests can launch turn-by-turn navigation on their smartphones with a single tap.

Packing & Weather Guide

Add a brief note on expected temperatures (daytime vs. cool desert/coastal evenings), power plug types, and any local cultural customs your guests will appreciate knowing in advance.
