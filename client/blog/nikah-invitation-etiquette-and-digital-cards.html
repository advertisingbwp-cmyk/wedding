Nikah Digital Card Etiquette: Duas, Modesty & Itineraries

The sacred Nikah contract is one of the most spiritually profound moments in Islamic tradition. Crafting a digital invitation that honors this religious sanctity while gracefully communicating contemporary event details requires intentional care and elegance.

1. Commencing with Bismillah and Quranic Verse

Traditional etiquette dictates beginning the invitation with Bismillah-ir-Rahman-ir-Rahim followed by an auspicious Quranic ayah, such as Surah An-Naba ("And We created you in pairs") or Surah Ar-Rum ("And among His signs is that He created for you spouses from among yourselves").

2. Clearly Distinguishing Dholki, Nikah, and Walima

A typical Muslim wedding encompasses:

  • Dholki / Mayun: Intimate family gathering with traditional folk songs, ubtan application, and sweet treats.
  • Mehendi: Vibrant henna evening with festive floral swing décor.
  • The Sacred Nikah: The solemn contract signing, khutbah, and ijab-e-qubool, often hosted in a mosque or banquet hall.
  • Rukhsati: The bride’s departure under the protection of the Holy Quran.
  • Walima Banquet: The feast hosted with gratitude by the groom's family.

3. Prayer Accommodations and Modest Attire

Your guests will deeply appreciate information on designated prayer spaces (for Maghrib and Isha prayers) and modesty dress recommendations (emerald, gold, and ivory formal attire).
