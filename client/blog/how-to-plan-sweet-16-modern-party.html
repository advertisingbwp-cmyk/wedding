How to Plan an Aesthetic Sweet 16 & 18th Birthday Website

Turning 16, 18, 21, or 30 is a milestone that calls for a party to remember. Paper cards are easily misplaced, and Instagram story invitations lack structure. A dedicated celebration website gives your friends a central hub for venue directions, dress codes, mocktail lists, and interactive features.

1. The Golden Date Reveal Scratch-Card

One of the biggest crowd-pleasers on Riwaayat Venue birthday websites is the interactive scratch card. Friends swipe across the screen to reveal the secret date and lounge venue before the countdown begins ticking down to party time.

2. Specifying the Vibe and Dress Code

Whether your theme is "Neon Glow," "Pastel Glam," or "Black Tie & Velvet," clearly outlining attire prevents guests from feeling underdressed or confused. Include color palette swatches right on the page!

3. Song Requests on the RSVP Form

Nothing gets people excited like having their favorite track played by the DJ. Use the custom question builder in your studio to let guests drop song suggestions when they RSVP.
