Help & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about customizing templates, collecting RSVPs, privacy modes, and guest coordination.

Can I preview a template before creating an account?
Yes! All 18 templates are 100% accessible via our live public preview. You can interact with the layout, test the date reveal, view sample ceremony itineraries, and explore the design before signing up.
How does the "Use This Template" onboarding work?
When you click "Use This Template", we remember your choice. If you aren't signed in, you can quickly sign in with Google or Email/Password. Immediately upon authentication, the system automatically clones the template into your account as a private Draft and opens the Live Studio editor. You never have to search for the template again.
Can I keep my celebration website completely private?
Yes. Riwaayat Venue offers three distinct privacy modes: Draft (only you can see it), Private Share (requires a custom passcode or individual guest invite link to view), and Public. Search engines cannot index your website while in Draft or Private Share.
Are guest phone numbers and meal choices kept private?
Yes, strictly. Guest RSVP records, phone numbers, and dietary preferences are stored in protected database subcollections and are visible only to the event owner in their secure dashboard. They are never exposed on the public website.
Can I add multiple ceremonies (Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Nikah, Walima)?
Yes. Every template comes pre-configured with culturally authentic ceremony defaults (such as Roka, Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Baraat, Pheras, Vidaai, or Dholki, Nikah, Rukhsati, Walima). In the studio, you can add, delete, rename, reorder, and set specific dress codes and venues for every ceremony.
What is the digital date reveal scratch card?
It is an interactive HTML5 canvas feature that mimics a real metallic foil lottery scratch-off card. Guests use their finger on mobile or mouse on desktop to scratch off the golden surface and reveal your auspicious wedding or party date.
How long does my event website stay active?
Forever. We do not charge recurring monthly hosting subscriptions. Once published, your website stays online as a digital keepsake of your celebration for you and your family to revisit for years to come.
Can I switch templates later without losing my content?
Yes! In the Live Studio editor, you can switch between any of our 18 templates anytime. Your names, story milestones, ceremony details, and photos will automatically adapt to the newly chosen theme aesthetic.
