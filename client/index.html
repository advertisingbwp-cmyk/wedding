Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Can I preview all templates before signing up?
Yes! You can preview all 18 templates live right in your browser with responsive desktop and mobile switches. You do not need to enter credit card details to browse or test templates.
How does the 1-Click "Use This Template" process work?
When you find a design you love, click "Use This Template". If you are not signed in, you will be invited to create your free account. Your chosen design is immediately cloned into your personal Live Studio with all ceremonies, photo slots, and RSVPs ready to personalize.
How does private passcode protection work?
In your event settings, you can choose "Private Share" mode and set a unique passcode. Guests visiting your link must enter the passcode to view private itineraries, maps, and submit RSVPs. Your event will never be indexed by Google.
Can guests RSVP for multiple separate ceremonies?
Yes. South Asian and multi-day celebrations often have different guest lists for Haldi, Sangeet, Nikah, and Reception. Riwaayat Venue lets you configure segmented RSVPs with dietary preferences and plus-one counts.
