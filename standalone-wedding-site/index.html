How It All Began

Our Love Story

From a casual hello to endless late-night calls and the magical sunset proposal. Here's a glimpse into the chapters that led us here.

Chapter I • October 2022

The Serendipitous Hello

Introduced by mutual close friends during Diwali festivities. Amid sparklers and sweet chatter, Vijay was captivated by Rashima's infectious laughter, and Rashima found Vijay's quiet wit irresistibly charming.

Chapter II • November 2022

The 4-Hour Coffee Date

What was planned as a quick 30-minute cappuccino turned into 4 hours of swapping childhood dreams, travel bucket lists, and shared playlists until the cafe staff began politely packing up chairs!

Chapter III • February 2025

The Sunset Proposal at Udaipur

Overlooking Lake Pichola as twilight turned the skies into shades of lavender and peach, Vijay knelt on one knee with a ring and asked for a lifetime of tomorrows. Through joyful tears and beaming smiles, Rashima said YES!

Chapter IV • December 2026

Forever Begins Today

With the blessings of our parents, the cheers of our friends, and our hearts completely intertwined, we take our sacred Saat Phere to start our forever chapter.
